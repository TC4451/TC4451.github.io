abstract: >-
  Pneumatic soft robots are typically fabricated by manual molding. We propose a low-cost approach to improve the print quality of desktop fused deposition modeling by adding a webcam that monitors printing and detects and corrects defects such as holes or gaps, improving the air-tightness of printed pneumatic actuators without fine-tuning printing parameters.
