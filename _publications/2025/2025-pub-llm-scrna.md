abstract: >-
  We present a framework that leverages gene-specific textual annotations from the NCBI Gene database to generate biologically contextualized cell embeddings. For each cell in an scRNA-seq dataset, genes are ranked by expression, their descriptions are embedded with large language models, and an expression-weighted average yields a compact, semantically rich representation for cell-type clustering, cell vulnerability dissection, and trajectory inference.
