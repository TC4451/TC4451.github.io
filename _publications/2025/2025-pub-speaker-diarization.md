abstract: >-
  We propose an LLM-assisted in-meeting speaker correction system that lets users fix speaker attribution errors through brief corrective feedback. After streaming ASR and diarization, the system presents concise LLM-generated summaries to help users spot important speaker errors, and incorporates feedback by updating the transcript and adding online speaker enrollments. On the AMI headset test set, it reduces DER by 31.99% and speaker substitution error by 52.68% relative to a streaming baseline.
