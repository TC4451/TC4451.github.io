abstract: >-
  DeformX couples a Cosserat rod physics engine with NVIDIA Isaac Sim to simulate deformable linear objects such as cables and ropes with physically faithful dynamics, self-collision, and contact with free-form meshes, while rendering them realistically through mesh skinning. Fine-tuning SAM3 on DeformX data improves real-image wire segmentation by 10.2% mAP@75, and a rope-swinging policy trained purely in simulation transfers to a real UR5e arm.
