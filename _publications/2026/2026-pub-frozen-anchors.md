abstract: >-
  Real-world machine learning applications often face both distribution shift and label noise. We study semantic representations from frozen language models as domain-invariant anchors, and use a weighted loss that adjusts each sample's contribution based on its similarity to the corresponding language anchor, making models more robust to noisy labels while generalizing across unseen domains.
