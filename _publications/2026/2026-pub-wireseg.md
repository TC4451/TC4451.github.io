abstract: >-
  Wires and cables are hard to segment because they are thin, highly deformable, and frequently self-occluded, and instance-level annotations are expensive. We present WireSeg-32K, a synthetic dataset with 32,000 RGB images, instance masks, depth maps, and a real-world test set, generated by coupling Cosserat-rod dynamics with photorealistic Isaac Sim rendering. LoRA fine-tuning SAM3 on WireSeg-32K alone improves real-world mAP@75 by 10.2%.
